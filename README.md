## Problem Statement

An online retail business generates a large amount of transactional data containing information about invoices, products, quantities, prices, customers, countries, and transaction dates.

The objective of this project is to analyze this data and answer important business questions such as:

* How much revenue is generated by the business?
* Which countries contribute the most revenue?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* How does revenue change over time?
* Which months show the strongest sales performance?
* How strongly are Quantity and Unit Price related to Revenue?
* Are there unusually large transactions or outliers?
* Which customer and sales patterns can support better business decisions?

## Data Inspection

Initial inspection identified:

* Original rows: **541,909**
* Duplicate rows: **5,268**
* Missing Description values: **1,454**
* Missing CustomerID values: **135,080**
* Negative Quantity records: **10,624**
* Zero Quantity records: **0**
* UnitPrice less than or equal to zero: **2,517**
* Cancelled invoice rows: **9,288**

After duplicate removal, **536,641 rows** remained.

Further investigation showed that negative quantities represented cancelled invoices, returns, damaged items, stock adjustments, missing stock, and other non-sales transactions.

---

## Data Cleaning Process

The following cleaning steps were performed:

1. Created a copy of the raw dataset to preserve the original data.
2. Removed exact duplicate records.
3. Investigated negative quantities and cancelled invoices.
4. Excluded cancelled transactions from the main sales analysis.
5. Excluded negative stock adjustment and return records.
6. Removed records with zero or negative UnitPrice.
7. Removed records with missing product descriptions.
8. Preserved missing CustomerID records for overall sales analysis.
9. Created a separate customer-level dataset using records where CustomerID was available.
10. Created a new Revenue field:

```python
Revenue = Quantity * UnitPrice
```

11. Created additional date-related features:

* Year
* Month
* Month Name
* Quarter
* Day
* Hour

### Final Cleaned Dataset

* **524,878 valid sales records**
* **392,692 customer-analysis records**

The cleaned dataset was saved for further EDA and Power BI analysis.

### Revenue Correlation

Revenue showed a strong positive relationship with Quantity:

* **Quantity vs Revenue correlation: 0.907**
* **UnitPrice vs Revenue correlation: 0.137**

This indicates that transaction revenue is influenced more strongly by the number of units purchased than by product price alone.
